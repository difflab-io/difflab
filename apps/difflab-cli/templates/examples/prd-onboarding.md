# Help new members finish project onboarding

- **Status:** draft
- **Owner:** Product team
- **Date:** 2026-01-14

## Summary

Give invited members a short checklist so they can find their project and complete their first task.

## Problem and Evidence

New members frequently ask where to start after accepting an invite. This is a product assumption until interviews confirm the pattern.

## Users and Use Cases

- New project member: find the project, read its purpose, and finish a first task.

## Goals

- Members can find their first task without contacting an administrator.

## Non-Goals

- Training on every product feature.

## Proposed Experience

After joining, a member sees a three-step checklist with links to the project overview and an assigned starter task. Dismissal is reversible from the help menu.

## Requirements

### Visible next step

Show one task assigned to the member, or a clear empty state when none exists.

**Acceptance:** A member with no assigned task sees an actionable empty state, not a blank panel.

## Success Measures

Measure the share of invited members who open a project and complete a task within seven days; record a baseline before launch.

## Risks and Dependencies

- Task assignment data may be delayed: refresh the checklist when assignment changes.

## Open Questions

- Should dismissed checklists reappear for new projects? Product owner to decide.

## Rollout

Enable for a small cohort, compare completion and feedback, then expand or disable.
